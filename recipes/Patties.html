Aloo Patties Recipe

Steps to make Aloo patties
  1. In a large bowl, mix the flour, salt, and 2 tbsp oil. Gradually add water and knead into a smooth, firm dough. Cover and let it rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan, sauté the cumin seeds, onions, and green chili until soft. Add the dry spices (coriander powder, chaat masala, salt, chili powder, and turmeric). Fold in the boiled mashed potatoes and green peas, mix well, and finish with a squeeze of lemon juice. Let the filling cool down completely.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the corn flour and oil (or ghee) until it forms a smooth paste
  4. Divide the dough into equal portions (about 5 balls). Roll each ball into a thin flat roti. Spread the corn flour slurry evenly over one roti, dust lightly with a pinch of dry flour, and place another roti on top. Repeat this stacking process.
  5. Roll the stacked rotis together slightly into a larger sheet, trim the uneven edges, and slice the sheet into even squares or rectangles. Place a spoonful of the cooled potato filling in the center of each square, apply a little water to the edges, and fold them into triangles or rectangles, pressing the forks along the edges to seal securely
  6. Heat oil in a kadai or pan over medium-low heat. Fry the patties slowly for 8 to 10 minutes, turning occasionally, until the external layers open up, turn golden brown, and become ultra-crispy.
